MRI Preprocessing, step by step

From raw DICOM to a smoothed, standard-space functional image — an interactive companion to Analysis and Processing of MRI Data, Exercise 1 — Preprocessing. All data below is real, de-identified, and freely redistributable (CC0), from one matched T1w + fMRI participant. Commands you type are simulated, not actually executed.

What this is

This page walks through the homework's 5-stage FSL preprocessing pipeline — DICOM→NIfTI, motion correction, brain extraction, registration and normalization, and smoothing — using one real, CC0-licensed participant's data: a 192-slice T1w MPRAGE and a 240-volume resting-state BOLD scan, from the same scan session.

  • Workflow — a clickable diagram of all 5 homework stages: why each step exists, what goes in and out, the exact command, and what to check afterward.
  • Terminal — pick a step and type the real FSL command yourself, on both real DICOM series. Correct commands reveal the actual precomputed output; your own filenames carry through to later steps.
  • Viewer — inspect anything you've revealed: slice through it, compare before/after, overlay a mask, or plot motion parameters. Nothing appears here until you've actually produced it.

The T1w (anatomical) and fMRI (functional) branches run in parallel from Stage 1, and join in Stage 4 when the functional data is registered to this participant's own T1w and then resampled into standard space — that's the diagram's cross-branch rows.

Data, licenses & provenance

Dataset

sub-01 — a single adult human brain, 3T Siemens Prisma. Published by its creator (dfsp-spirit) at github.com/dfsp-spirit/sub-01, backed by a Zenodo record: MR scans of a human, adult brain at 3T in BIDS format. Includes DICOMS, preprocessed output of FreeSurfer, fmriprep and qsirecon, doi:10.5281/zenodo.22697454. License: CC0 1.0 (public domain dedication — no restrictions, no attribution required; we credit it anyway).

This exercise uses two of its DICOM series, from the same participant, same scan session (verified directly: both series share one DICOM StudyInstanceUID) — series 0002, a 192-slice T1w MPRAGE (1mm isotropic), and series 0005, a 240-volume resting-state BOLD EPI (3mm). Both are real, unmodified scanner DICOM files, downloadable in full from the Files panel in the Terminal tab.

Headers: already anonymized by the publisher with dicom-anonymizer before release — PatientName/PatientID literally read ANONYMIZED, birth date and study date are reset, and institution/physician/device-serial fields are all empty. Verified directly against the downloaded files, all 432 of them, not just a sample.

What we changed before publishing: the T1w image was defaced with FSL's fsl_deface (verified: zero overlap between the removed voxels and the BET brain mask — see docs/manifest.md). The raw DICOM pixel data (not defaced) is not published here beyond what the original CC0 release already makes public; our derived NIfTI outputs use the defaced version throughout.
